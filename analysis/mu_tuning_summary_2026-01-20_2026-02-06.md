## Evidence Analysis
Source: `analysis/mu_trades_baseline_2026-01-20_2026-02-06.json`

Observed across baseline trades:
- Most losses came from `trailing_stop`, `stop_loss`, and `adverse_flow` exits.
- L2 confirmation gates were permissive (`min_imbalance=0.04`, `min_signed_aggression=0.03`), so many weak-flow entries passed.
- Sweep on recorded trade-level L2 metrics showed a stable improvement region when requiring stronger directional flow conviction.

Selected robust gates:
- `l2_min_imbalance = 0.12`
- `l2_min_signed_aggression = 0.12`
- Kept other MU L2 constraints unchanged.
